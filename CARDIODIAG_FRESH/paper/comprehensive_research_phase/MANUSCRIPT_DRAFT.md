# CARDIODIAG Comprehensive Research Phase

## Abstract

CARDIODIAG evaluated a small angiographic CAD cohort using a prespecified five-feature model and repeated nested validation. A fully nested calibration procedure removed selection-induced calibration bias identified in an earlier version. A bounded optimization search did not improve the corrected baseline. Exploratory symptom, ECG, laboratory and medical-history groups showed higher apparent performance, but prediction-time timing and measurement documentation were insufficient for clinical eligibility; these results are hypothesis-generating only. No compatible untouched external validation cohort was available.

## Conclusions

The V3 logistic regression baseline remains the preferred internal-validation reference, with substantial limitations. The study is not ready for clinical deployment or publication as a validated clinical tool without governance documentation and independent external validation.
