# Phase 2 Final Assessment

1. Best defensible stage: Stage 1 strict baseline, because it is the only stage with a frozen, defensible feature set; exploratory Stage 2 symptom performance is higher but not eligible for confirmation.
2. Genuinely eligible features: Age, Sex, Current Smoker, EX-Smoker and FH, with source timing limitations still documented.
3. Symptom improvement: apparent improvement in exploratory analysis (ROC-AUC about 0.876 versus 0.734 baseline), but not clinically confirmatory because timing/coding are unresolved.
4. ECG improvement: apparent improvement (about 0.795), but not confirmatory because acquisition timing is undocumented.
5. Statistical/clinical meaning: no confirmatory superiority claim; clinical meaning is unresolved without timing evidence, larger sample and external validation.
6. Best balance: V3 logistic regression; unweighted gives the best probability quality, balanced gives a more favorable fixed-threshold operating point.
7. Cohort sufficiency: insufficient for deployment, external validity, or precise calibration claims; suitable for methodological internal validation.
8. Strongest contribution: reproducible correction of calibration-selection bias plus transparent feature-stage and dataset-compatibility audit.
9. Publication blockers: governance documentation, source timing/units, independent compatible validation and stronger clinical utility evidence.
10. Exact next step: collect or obtain an independent cohort with the same five predictors, documented pre-angiography timing, compatible angiographic endpoint and patient-level independence.

Recommendation: CONDITIONAL GO for the next data-collection and external-validation milestone; NO-GO for clinical deployment or a claim of validated clinical utility.
