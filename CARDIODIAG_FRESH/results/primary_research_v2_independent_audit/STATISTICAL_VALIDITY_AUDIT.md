# Statistical Validity Audit

## Classification: Correct with limitations

The comparison table uses matched source rows and paired bootstrap differences. No confirmatory p-values or ordinary independent-fold tests are reported, and multiplicity is explicitly not applied. The comparison intervals are descriptive conditional resampling summaries, not population confidence intervals. Repeated cross-validation reuses participants and model selection/calibration adds further dependence. Comparisons should remain descriptive unless a prespecified dependent-CV method is justified in a future analysis.
