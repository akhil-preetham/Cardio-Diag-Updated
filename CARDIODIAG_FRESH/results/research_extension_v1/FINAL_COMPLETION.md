## Scope boundary

This is completed retrospective computational research. Patient independence, predictor timing, target code ascertainment and exact outcome equivalence remain limited; clinical utility and prospective validation were not established. Author/institutional declarations and venue-specific submission work remain separate from completed experiments. The project must not be advertised as a universally reliable diagnostic system or as accepted for publication.
