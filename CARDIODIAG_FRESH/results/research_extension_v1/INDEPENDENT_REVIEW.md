## AngioCAD raw mapping and endpoint checks

The reviewer independently opened the unchanged downloaded official XLSX files with pandas/openpyxl rather than reusing the screening XML parser. This confirms 377 unique feature IDs, 413 unique label IDs, 377 matched cases, no feature-only cases and 36 label-only cases. No missing vessel severity labels exist among all 413 label records. The observed severity vocabulary is exactly NL, 1-25, 26-50, 51-75, 76-90, 91-99 and 100. The 15 vessel labels embedded in the feature workbook agree with the separate label workbook on every matched ID after value-string normalization; dtype differences alone are not label disagreement.

The patient-level operational >50% rule (any segment 51-75/76-90/91-99/100) produces 298 positive and 79 nonpositive cases in the matched feature cohort. Ten of the 79 have a 26-50 segment. These are boundary-ambiguous for ZAS's >=50% definition; a 26-50 category cannot distinguish exact 50 from lower stenosis. A sensitivity analysis assigning those ten positive is a bound under a changed endpoint, not reconstruction of the true >=50% target.

The 'features' workbook itself contains diagnostic outcomes and scores. Use an explicit predictor allowlist; do not feed the whole workbook into a generic feature selector. ID, artery stenosis, Gensini score, diagnosis and angiographic series information must not enter the shared clinical core. The raw Sex vocabulary is Female/Male; DM and HTN are binary 0/1. The ZAS spelling Fmale should be mapped explicitly. Shared core Age/Sex/DM/HTN/Current Smoker has no unit rescaling requirement; Age has 6 missing AngioCAD values and Current Smoker has 46. Training-source imputation must be retained when transporting a source-trained model; absent smoker values must not be silently filled with 'no'.

Clinical record IDs are unique release case keys, not proof of unique patients. No feature-row duplicate is present after removing ID. Sixteen fields can be mapped by names across ZAS/AngioCAD, but name mapping does not establish laboratory unit/timing equivalence. A 16-field fingerprint comparison has zero exact cross-cohort collisions; a five-field Age/Sex/DM/HTN/Current Smoker comparison has 93 distinct common combinations. The low-dimensional collisions are plausible chance matches, not evidence that 93 patients overlap. Conversely, no 16-field collision does not prove independent patient provenance. Do not deduplicate different cohorts using the five-field fingerprint.

## Completed prefit experimental code review

The reviewer read the complete initial `runner/research_extension_v1/zas.py`, its frozen-protocol source text and `transport.py` before model fitting. No blocking label-leakage or partition-mapping defect was found. This review does not prove absence of every possible implementation defect or validate the clinical task.

ZAS: each outer-training calibration block C has a disjoint development complement D. The entire 30-candidate search, including inner tuning and preprocessing, runs inside D. A retained D-refit model scores C; the sigmoid fitted on C stays paired with that exact D-refit model for outer scoring. Inner membership positions correctly map from D to outer-training source-row IDs. All three retained components are averaged. The uncalibrated comparison averages probabilities of those identical three base models, which isolates the calibration operation more cleanly than comparing to a different full-training model. Calibration-block labels never enter the base search and outer-test labels are absent from its training API. The 120 hyperparameter settings per component and 390 fits including candidate refits are consistent with the grids.

The all-38-field control and information ablations deliberately retain the historical calibration construction; protocol/model IDs disclose this limitation. This is appropriate for matching the historical selection procedure, but these controls must not be described as independently calibrated. The full procedure remains a retrospectively motivated sensitivity analysis on previously examined source-row partitions. Small calibration blocks and weakly regularized raw-probability sigmoids can be unstable, and declines/warnings must remain in the reported results.

Transport: an entire `GridSearchCV` is the estimator passed to `CalibratedClassifierCV(ensemble=True)`. Its per-calibration training clone selects C only inside its own development rows and remains paired with the corresponding calibration model. The raw arm refits on all source rows; thus raw-versus-calibrated differences also reflect training size and averaging, not only calibration. The runner discloses that confounding. Target missing predictors are processed by source-fitted imputers. Target feature values or labels do not select a C, calibrator, threshold or predictor set. Target probabilities/models are saved before outcome scoring. The endpoint rules correctly distinguish >50%, exclusion of the ten boundary-ambiguous cases, and upper-bound assignment. Binary0/1 semantics, recording timing, patient independence and cohort/outcome equivalence remain explicit assumptions/limitations. The final MCAR addition was inspected after completion: it draws one fixed source-test-cell uniform matrix per outer partition, applies nested fixed-rate masks, and predicts with the existing raw-arm fit without any refitting. The initial and final prefit protocol records are retained separately; the frozen final source hash matches the completed implementation.

The research-only scoring entry point `predict_research.py` was also read. It requires exactly the five core columns, rejects invalid binary/sex labels and fully missing rows, checks a fixed local bundle hash before joblib loading, refuses to overwrite an output, and reports missingness/source-age-range flags without diagnostic labels. These are software input checks, not per-record reliability certification or a general distribution-shift detector.
