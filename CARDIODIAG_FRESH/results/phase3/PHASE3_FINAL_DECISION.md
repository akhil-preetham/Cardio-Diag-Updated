# Phase 3 Final Decision

Best available endpoint candidate: the 2026 angiographic stenosis dataset (100 patients) is a high-priority source-access candidate because its public description reports angiographic/functionally confirmed stenosis, but its five predictors, timing, patient-level table and access terms remain unverified. MultiD4CAD and CADICA are real CAD resources but are multimodal/image-focused and do not verify strict predictor equivalence. Cleveland remains the closest locally available endpoint but fails strict predictor availability and independence constraints.

External validation is not feasible now. The missing requirements are an independent patient-level cohort, all five baseline predictors, documented pre-angiography timing, compatible outcome adjudication, data dictionary, governance approval and adequate sample size. Recommendation: CONDITIONAL GO for institutional cohort acquisition; NO-GO for claiming external validation or clinical deployment.
