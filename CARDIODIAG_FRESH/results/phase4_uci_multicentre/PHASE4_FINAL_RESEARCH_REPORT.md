# Phase 4 final research report

The official four-centre cohort was reconstructed at **920 records**. All centres have both outcome classes, so four-fold IECV is feasible as a descriptive transportability study. The strict feature set is defensible before angiography but does not reproduce V3's five predictors. The UCI endpoint is related angiographic disease status, not claimed identical to Z-Alizadeh Cath.

The primary strict logistic pooled out-of-centre result is available in `POOLED_OUT_OF_CENTRE_METRICS.csv`; centre-level results are in `CENTRE_VALIDATION_METRICS.csv`. Calibration and discrimination are reported separately. The principal limitations are missingness, lack of patient identifiers, incomplete timing provenance, centre selection and small-centre uncertainty.
