# Phase 6 statistical validity review

## Overall assessment

**The outer discrimination comparison is methodologically defensible for the narrow estimand of retrospective, source-row discrimination on this ZAS dataset, with substantial limitations.** It is not a patient-level, prospective, external-validation or clinical-utility evaluation. The evidence supports the descriptive statement that the selection-aware procedure had higher repeat-level out-of-fold ROC-AUC than the re-evaluated V3-style comparator on all three frozen repeat partitions. It does not support inferential significance or population generalization.

### Confirmed defects versus limitations

- **Confirmed reporting defect (not an evaluation defect):** Phase 6 calibration selection metadata lists four folds although the operational cross-fit uses three. Phase 6.1 found the extra entry duplicates metadata and does not enter fitting or predictions. See `results/phase6_1_audit/PHASE6_IMPLEMENTATION_AUDIT.md`, “Calibration metadata count.”
- **Calibration limitation:** winner and hyperparameters were selected using labels from all inner folds before those same rows were used in calibration cross-fitting. This makes calibration scores selection-dependent. Inner score models train on about two-thirds of outer training rows, while final model trains on all outer-training rows. This does not reveal outer-test labels and does not invalidate the outer discrimination evaluation. See `results/phase6_1_audit/PHASE6_CALIBRATION_AUDIT.md`, “Calibration limitations.”
- **Three-repeat limitation:** the full 25-outer-fold plan exceeded 35 CPU minutes without producing artifacts; only first three frozen repeats were analyzed. The 15 folds are not 15 independent datasets; repeats reuse source rows. Claims must remain descriptive and within-dataset. See `results/phase6_zas_selection/SELECTION_AWARE_PROTOCOL.md`, “Split and nested selection,” and `PHASE6_FINAL_DECISION.md`.
- **Identity limitation:** source-row indexes, not verified unique patient identifiers, are the unit. Duplicate patients or multiple records cannot be ruled out. See `results/phase6_zas_selection/PHASE6_MANIFEST.json` (`patient_identifier_verified:false`) and `PHASE6_FINAL_DECISION.md`.
- **Outer X inspection:** post-fit permutation importance uses outer-test features and labels after predictions are frozen. It is not fed back into candidate selection, but those test folds are consequently not untouched by every secondary analysis. Keep importance exploratory and do not imply it is a second independent validation. See `runner/phase6_zas_selection/run.py`, permutation-analysis block in `run()`, and `results/phase6_1_audit/PHASE6_CALIBRATION_AUDIT.md`.

## Model comparison interpretation

Use: “Across the three evaluated repeat partitions, the selection-aware procedure had higher complete-repeat OOF ROC-AUC than the matched, re-evaluated V3-style comparator.” Give each repeat difference (+0.1764, +0.1579, +0.1877) and the mean (+0.1740) descriptively. Do not call this a statistically significant or patient-level improvement. The comparator is not the historical V3 predictor frozen with its original fitted parameters/calibrator/threshold; name it as the Phase 6 V3-style comparator.

No generalization beyond these source rows is established. Feature timing is unresolved, and there is no independent compatible ZAS cohort. The Phase 5 0.928 AUC is not evidence for this claim; Phase 5.1/7.1 classify it as selected post hoc.
