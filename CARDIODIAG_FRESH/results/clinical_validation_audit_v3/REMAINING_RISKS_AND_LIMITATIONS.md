# Remaining Risks and Limitations

- Z-Alizadeh feature timing and units are incompletely documented; downstream echocardiography/catheterization variables require exclusion or clinical review.
- Z-Alizadeh is a small single-cohort dataset; internal validation uncertainty may be large.
- NHANES is cross-sectional, uses self-reported and measurement-defined variables, and requires survey design variables and component-specific weights.
- PTB-XL and PTB Diagnostic have related PTB provenance and unresolved patient overlap.
- Statlog is an exact predictor-level/label-corresponding match to the Cleveland records under the documented normalization; Five States, Mendeley, and Kaggle independence is not established.
- Kaggle licensing and Five States provenance remain unresolved.
- No clinical deployment, clinical utility, ethics, or generalizability claim is supported by this audit.
