# Literature Review 2024-2026

- Collins et al. TRIPOD+AI, BMJ 2024;385:e078378, DOI: https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2023-078378. This replaces TRIPOD 2015 for transparent reporting of regression and machine-learning prediction models.
- Collins et al. Evaluation of clinical prediction models, part 1, BMJ 2024;384:e074819, DOI: https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2023-074819. It emphasizes internal, internal-external and external validation and generalisability.
- Riley et al. Evaluation of clinical prediction models, part 2, BMJ 2024;384:e074820, DOI: https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2023-074820. It provides guidance for external validation and calibration assessment.
- Moons et al. PROBAST+AI, BMJ 2025;388:e082505, DOI: https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2024-082505. It updates risk-of-bias and applicability assessment for regression and AI prediction models.

These sources support the conservative interpretation used here: small internal-validation studies require transparent predictor timing, separation of development and validation, calibration assessment, and external validation before claims of clinical usefulness.
