# Final Research Protocol

The primary cohort is Z-Alizadeh Sani with Cath as the angiographic CAD target and five strict predictors. The primary validation is five repeated five-fold outer cross-validation with four-fold inner selection using the exact Stage 6 manifest. V3 fully nested calibration is the preferred architecture. V4 compact optimization and the feature-group experiment are secondary analyses. No outer-test labels may influence preprocessing, feature selection, hyperparameter selection, calibration, threshold selection or model choice.
