# Research Contribution Assessment

Current contribution: a reproducible methodological and dataset-quality case study, not a clinically deployable prediction model. Verified findings include the V3 fully nested calibration correction, the negative V4 optimization result, confirmed Cleveland-Statlog overlap, and exploratory feature-group gains that remain clinically non-eligible. The strongest next contribution is a prospectively documented cohort with the same five predictors and compatible CAD ascertainment, followed by untouched external validation.
