# Phase 5 reliability report

Calibration, uncertainty, selective prediction, OOD and decision-curve outputs were generated from saved centre-held-out predictions. Thresholds for selective prediction and decision curves were prespecified descriptive grids, not optimized from held-out labels. OOD scores are covariate-shift diagnostics rather than disease probabilities. Decision curves are not interpreted as clinical utility because no decision threshold was prespecified for deployment.
