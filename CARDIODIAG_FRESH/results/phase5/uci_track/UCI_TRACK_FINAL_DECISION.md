# UCI Phase 5 track decision: CONDITIONAL GO

Stage 2 and Stage 4 remain separate prediction-time tasks. The new Phase 5 benchmark executes regularized logistic regression; historical Phase 4 random-forest and histogram-gradient results remain available as comparators. Centre-held-out validation preserves the outer centre and uses fold-local preprocessing. The UCI endpoint is related to, but not asserted identical with, the ZAS angiographic endpoint. Centre variation, missingness and Switzerland instability prevent clinical or external-validation claims.
