# ZAS Phase 5 track decision: CONDITIONAL GO

The frozen V3 five-predictor track is preserved. Feature-group and bounded model comparisons are out-of-fold and fold-local. Echo/diagnostic timing remains uncertain and those fields are excluded from the eligible combined group. Optional GAM, EBM and Bayesian models were not executed because their packages are unavailable. No candidate is selected solely by pooled ROC-AUC; calibration, feature timing, and stability remain required.
