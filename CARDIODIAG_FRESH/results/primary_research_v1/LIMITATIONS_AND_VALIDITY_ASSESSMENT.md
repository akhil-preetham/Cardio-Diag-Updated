# Limitations and Validity Assessment

- The cohort contains 303 records from one source and is not an independent population sample.
- The strict feature set is deliberately conservative because timing and downstream diagnostic status are incompletely documented.
- Repeated CV predictions are dependent; bootstrap intervals are resampling summaries, not external-population confidence intervals.
- No model was selected using pooled outer-test performance; the displayed ranking is descriptive.
- Cleveland/Statlog are confirmed overlapping records, and no external validation claim is made.
- Clinical utility, prospective prediction, fairness, and deployment performance were not established.
- Failed model-fold records: 0.
