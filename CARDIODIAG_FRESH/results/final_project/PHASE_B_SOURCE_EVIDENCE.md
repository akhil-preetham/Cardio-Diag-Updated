# Phase B — Public source documentation

UCI's Z-Alizadeh Sani card states 303 instances, collection for CAD diagnosis, and a binary CAD/Normal definition at >=50% narrowing; its variable table is blank in the page snapshot. The UCI Heart Disease card identifies four databases and gives definitions for the 14 commonly used attributes, including admission resting BP, serum cholesterol, fasting glucose threshold, exercise variables and angiographic `ca`/`num`. These definitions do not provide row-level timing. References: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/412/z and https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/45/heart+disease (accessed 2026-11-13).

Local ZAS variable labels and prior clinical audits provide meanings for candidate features, but several units, coding definitions, ascertainment timing and measurement context remain unverified. Those fields are explicitly marked in the CSV matrix; no clinical timing claim is upgraded from plausibility alone.
