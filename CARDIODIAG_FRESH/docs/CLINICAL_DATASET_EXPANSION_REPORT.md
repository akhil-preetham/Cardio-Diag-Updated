# CARDIODIAG Clinical Dataset Expansion Report

This v2 expansion acquires Z-Alizadeh Sani, selected public NHANES 2017-2018 components, and the official PTB-XL and PTB Diagnostic archives. It indexes ECG metadata without extracting waveforms and catalogs Auricle without downloading its 7.3 GB archive. No model training or performance evaluation was performed.

Target definitions remain separate: CAD, general cardiovascular history, ECG diagnosis, multimodal physiology, and mortality are not interchangeable.
