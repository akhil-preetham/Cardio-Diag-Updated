# Institutional Cohort Acquisition Protocol

## Population
Adults undergoing clinically indicated coronary angiography for suspected CAD. Record consecutive or prespecified eligible cases and document referral spectrum.

## Outcome
Binary CAD defined before data lock as at least 50% diameter stenosis in a prespecified major coronary vessel, preferably by quantitative coronary analysis; record vessel, measurement method, threshold and functional adjudication for borderline lesions.

## Predictors
Age, sex, current smoker, former smoker and family history of CAD. Record definitions, source, coding, missingness and the exact time each variable was known relative to angiography.

## Independence and governance
Use a new institution or time period with patient-level deduplication. Obtain IRB/ethics approval or formal exemption, a data-use agreement, deidentification certification, secure storage, access logging and an analysis plan approved before external labels are opened.

## Minimum cohort
The cohort must support at least several hundred CAD-positive and CAD-negative patients for useful calibration and threshold precision; final size must use the Riley external-validation framework rather than a universal rule.
