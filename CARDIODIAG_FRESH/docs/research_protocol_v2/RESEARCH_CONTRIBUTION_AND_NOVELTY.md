# Research Contribution and Novelty

## Demonstrated

The project demonstrates a reproducible audit trail showing that strict feature
eligibility materially changes the defensible CARDIODIAG problem. It also demonstrates
that Cleveland and Statlog cannot be treated as independent validation cohorts and that
historical high performance should not be interpreted without leakage controls.

## Not demonstrated

The project has not demonstrated a new algorithm, clinical utility, prospective
prediction, superiority over published methods, external generalization, or causal
importance. Combining standard models, calibration, cross-validation, and explainability
is not by itself novel.

## Defensible direction

The most defensible contribution is methodological transparency: a small-cohort CAD
classification benchmark with explicit downstream-feature exclusion, reproducible
nested validation, calibrated probability reporting, and honest uncertainty boundaries.
Future claims require an independent target-compatible cohort and a prespecified
calibration/uncertainty design.
