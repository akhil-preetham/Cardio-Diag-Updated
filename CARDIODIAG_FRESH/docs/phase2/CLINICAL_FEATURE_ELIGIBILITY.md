# Clinical Feature Eligibility

Only Age, Sex, Current Smoker, EX-Smoker and FH remain the verified primary feature set. Other features are classified as potentially pre-test but require timing, ascertainment or unit confirmation. Symptom, ECG and laboratory groups are exploratory only. EF-TTE, Region RWMA, VHD and Cath are excluded from the intended pre-test model. The dataset does not document feature acquisition time sufficiently to convert plausible clinical timing into verified eligibility.
