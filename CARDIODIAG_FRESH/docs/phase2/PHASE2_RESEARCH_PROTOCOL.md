# Phase 2 Research Protocol

Stage 1 is the strict five-feature CAD risk baseline. Stage 2 evaluates symptoms only as exploratory presentation information. Stage 3 evaluates ECG and laboratory groups only as exploratory initial-investigation information. Stage 4 is reserved for later diagnostic variables and is not a pre-test model. Exact Stage 6 repeated nested splits and V3 fully nested calibration are retained. All preprocessing and model selection remain within training partitions. No external validation claim is permitted.
