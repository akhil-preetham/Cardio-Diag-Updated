# Clinical Dataset Limitations v2

PTB-XL and PTB Diagnostic archives are preserved and ZIP-validated, but their waveforms were not extracted or loaded into memory. Auricle full acquisition was explicitly deferred because of its 7.3 GB compressed/15.4 GB uncompressed footprint. NHANES is cross-sectional survey data and must not be described as prospective disease prediction. Cross-source patient overlap remains unresolved where identifiers are unavailable.
