# Dataset and methodology source review

Reviewed 14 November 2026. This is a data-feasibility plan, not evidence that a new cohort has been evaluated by a model. Provider metadata and the two small AngioCAD workbooks have now been acquired unchanged into `data_screening/angiocad/`, with checksums and a reproducible screening script. Reports print aggregate counts only. Image archives were not downloaded, and no model was fitted.

## Recommended data strategy

Keep **ZAS retrospective angiography-defined CAD discrimination** as the main existing study. First investigate **AngioCAD** as a genuinely independent clinical-tabular cohort. It is the most promising newly identified candidate in this search, but its label distribution, field definitions, acquisition timing and overlap with ZAS must pass a compatibility audit before it can be called external validation. Prepare a prespecified shared-feature procedure using development data only; do not select a winning feature mapping after seeing the candidate cohort's outcomes.

Keep **UCI four-centre internal-external evaluation** as a separately stated transportability experiment. Four observed historical centres do not prove reliability in arbitrary hospitals, and UCI does not validate the historical 38-field ZAS model merely because both concern coronary disease. If external compatibility fails, a transparent single-resource reanalysis plus a separately labelled centre-shift study remains a legitimate bounded output. It is scientifically preferable to an invented external-validation claim.

## AngioCAD workbook feasibility check (aggregate, not modeling)

Acquisition and screening are reproducible in [the screening report](data_screening/angiocad/SCREENING_REPORT.md), [screening script](data_screening/angiocad/screen_angiocad.py), [aggregate JSON](data_screening/angiocad/aggregate_screening.json) and [provenance manifest](data_screening/angiocad/PROVENANCE_MANIFEST.json). Direct official workbook links are https://zenodo.org/api/records/15826856/files/AngioCAD_Features.xlsx/content and https://zenodo.org/api/records/15826856/files/AngioCAD_Labels.xlsx/content. Provider MD5, byte sizes and local SHA-256 were checked.

The official two workbooks were read in memory using their Zenodo file URLs. Nonblank ID rows establish **377 unique clinical-feature cases**, **413 unique label cases**, and **377 matched IDs**. Thus the paper/record's 413 cases must not be used as the available complete tabular cohort size.

Using the explicitly provisional rule that any segment labelled `51-75`, `76-90`, `91-99`, or `100` is positive yields **298 positive and 79 non-obstructive cases** among the matched 377 (324/89 in all 413 label records). The `26-50` category prevents exact reconstruction of ZAS's **>=50%** threshold: this rule is **>50%**. Among the 79 nonpositive cases, 10 have a 26-50 segment and are boundary-ambiguous for >=50; assigning all 10 positive would be an upper-bound sensitivity assignment, not a true >=50 endpoint. Non-obstructive controls are not necessarily healthy/normal.

Clinical diagnosis counts among the 377 rows are STEMI 186, NSTEMI 20, UA 55 and SA 116. This is an acute/stable coronary referral spectrum, not a general-population screening cohort. External transport results, even after careful harmonization, would concern this setting.

Potential shared predictors include Age, Sex, DM, HTN, Current Smoker, TG, Cholesterol, HDL, LDL, FBS, Cr, BUN, Hgb, leukocytes, Neut, Lymph and PLT. Units, timing and encodings have not yet been verified. FH, EX-Smoker and ZAS's symptom/ECG groups are absent from the header. **The exact V3 and 38-field ZAS models therefore cannot be transported unchanged.** A newly frozen reduced shared-feature procedure would be needed.

Exclude ID, coronary series, dominance unless clinically justified at the index time, Diagnosis, Gensini Score, vessel stenosis fields, LVEF and LVEF dysfunction from an intended pre-angiography feature set. Other medication/prior-disease fields also need timing review. Do not allow the outcome-defining artery labels or diagnostic scores into predictors. These exclusions are proposed protocol decisions, not a fitted analysis.

## Acquisition and compatibility gates

1. Freeze target population and index time: patients undergoing coronary evaluation, recorded pre-index information if it can be established; otherwise explicitly retrospective.
2. Freeze endpoint/adjudication: a patient-level binary rule with documented coronary stenosis cutoff, vessel rule and reference standard. Flag CCTA versus invasive angiography and functional-ischemia differences. Do not equate broad 'heart disease', event incidence and death.
3. Create a field-level matrix: name, definition, unit, encoding, acquisition time, missingness mechanism, outcome relation, and allowed mapping for every cohort. Map current smoking and former smoking separately only if source definitions support it. Absence of a field is not a negative clinical answer.
4. Establish entity and provenance: unique case/patient keys, repeated visits, image-to-patient links, duplicate records and cross-source reuse. Existing Heart5/Kaggle amalgams can overlap UCI/Statlog; they are not automatically independent cohorts.
5. Lock the shared-feature development analysis before examining external outcome associations. A reduced shared-feature model is a new development procedure; it is not external validation of the original 38-feature model if its required fields are absent.
6. Size evaluation for precision of discrimination and calibration, using anticipated event rate and score distribution. Do not declare any universal minimum count adequate. The two very small candidate cohorts may permit only preliminary evaluation with wide uncertainty.
7. Evaluate a locked model first. Any target-cohort recalibration must use a separate labelled adaptation subset; the evaluation subset remains untouched. Report original transport performance and updated performance separately.
8. Preserve access/version/hash metadata and all exclusions. Publish allowed derived artifacts, code, synthetic schema examples and predictions only to the extent permitted by data terms and privacy review.

## Primary and backup execution plan

**Primary, no new cohort dependence:** complete provenance audit and selection-independent calibration design for ZAS; compare the current historical procedure to corrected, frozen alternatives on explicitly reused source-row splits. Label this a methodological sensitivity/reanalysis because investigators already know dataset performance. Add prespecified predictor-group ablations, missing-feature sensitivity and probability-stability summaries only when they answer an objective. Avoid a growing model leaderboard.

**High-value extension:** review only AngioCAD's small clinical/label workbooks first. If a compatible shared-feature CAD endpoint and both classes exist, freeze a reduced source-trained model and assess transport on the independent cohort. If definitions differ, present a cross-cohort methodological replication with separate targets, not external validation. Do not download ~16 GB imagery for this tabular project.

**Backup extension:** use controlled MultiD4CAD if the authors/institution can complete the DUA and its endpoint/predictor compatibility passes. A single small external cohort may provide useful failure information, but cannot guarantee performance in diverse settings.

**Fallback if compatibility fails:** keep UCI as a separate centre-shift experiment, report all centre results and their instability, and write a transparent methodological paper without an external-generalization claim. A negative or uncertain external result can improve the paper's credibility when reported fully.

| [Official scikit-learn CalibratedClassifierCV documentation](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.calibration.CalibratedClassifierCV.html) | Calibration partitions must be chosen carefully. These methods are established. The paper-worthy contribution would be a specific, transparent empirical study of selection, calibration and transportability in these datasets, plus reusable verified artifacts; it is not a claim to have invented nested validation, calibration or internal-external validation.

## Source-check limitations

AngioCAD Zenodo metadata and file listings were verified through the official API at https://zenodo.org/api/records/15826856 after the web reader could not open its record. The API reports publication date 2025-07-15 (dataset release) while the linked paper is a 2026 publication. Workbook schema and aggregate label counts were then inspected in memory as detailed above; full missingness, units, clinical timing, duplicate-content and measurement validity remain unchecked. Kaggle license metadata were checked through its official datasets list endpoint; Unknown means unresolved, not prohibited or licensed by inference. All other facts above are grounded in the linked provider pages or original data/methodology publications; not in third-party dataset aggregators.
